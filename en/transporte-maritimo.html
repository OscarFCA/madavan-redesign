Modes

Three ways to move your cargo by sea.

We choose the mode based on volume, cargo type and the transit time you need, with competitive rates in every case.

FCL

Full container load

Standard-size containers for a single shipper, ready to continue by rail or truck in a multimodal setup.

LCL

Consolidated cargo

Share container space with other shipments when your volume doesn't fill a full unit. From one cubic metre.

BULK

Bulk cargo

Large volumes of commodities such as coal, grain and minerals in separate holds below deck.
