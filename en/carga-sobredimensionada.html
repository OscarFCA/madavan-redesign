How we work

From the drawing to the assembly.

We are verified experts in coordinating oversized cargo logistics, including the specification, type and quantity of equipment for each requirement.

01

Cargo specification

We review the dimensions, weight, centre of gravity and lifting points of the piece.

02

Equipment selection

We define the type and quantity of equipment: vessel, flat rack, lowboy, modular trailers or cranes.

03

Route and manoeuvres

We plan the route, the permits and the loading and unloading manoeuvres.

04

Transport and assembly

We execute the move with tracking and, if you need it, on-site assembly.
